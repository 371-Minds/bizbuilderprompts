What is JSON Prompting? 
JSON prompting isn’t some mystical AI spell — it’s just a more structured way to talk to AI 
video generators like Google VEO 3. 
Instead of giving a single messy sentence like: 
“Make a drink ad that explodes with particles and lands on a rock.” 
You give the AI a clear breakdown of exactly what to show, how it moves, what it looks like, and 
how the scene should unfold. Like this: 
{ 
"product_name": "Blaze Energy", 
"description": "A glowing can forms from swirling liquid, lands with impact, and ripples energy 
across a mountain cliff.", 
"style": "cinematic, slow-mo", 
"camera": "orbit shot, macro close-up", 
"motion": "assembles mid-air, lands with energy pulse", 
... 
}
