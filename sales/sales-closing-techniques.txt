Sales Closing Techniques

Here are 5 powerful closing techniques for sales calls:

1. **Assumptive Close**: "When would you like to get started with the implementation?"

2. **Alternative Close**: "Would you prefer the standard package or the premium package?"

3. **Urgency Close**: "This special offer expires at the end of the month. Should we move forward?"

4. **Summary Close**: Summarize all benefits discussed and ask for commitment.

5. **Question Close**: "What questions do you have before we proceed?"

Remember to:
- Build rapport first
- Address all objections
- Use confident body language
- Listen actively to customer needs

These techniques work best when you've established trust and demonstrated clear value.
