Business Strategy Framework

## Strategic Planning Template

### 1. Vision & Mission
- **Vision Statement**: Where do you want your business to be in 5 years?
- **Mission Statement**: What is your business's core purpose?
- **Values**: What principles guide your business decisions?

### 2. Market Analysis
- **Target Market**: Who are your ideal customers?
- **Market Size**: What's the total addressable market?
- **Competition**: Who are your main competitors and what are their strengths/weaknesses?
- **Market Trends**: What trends are shaping your industry?

### 3. SWOT Analysis
- **Strengths**: Internal factors that give you an advantage
- **Weaknesses**: Internal factors that put you at a disadvantage  
- **Opportunities**: External factors you could exploit
- **Threats**: External factors that could harm your business

### 4. Strategic Objectives
- **Financial Goals**: Revenue, profit, growth targets
- **Operational Goals**: Efficiency, quality, process improvements
- **Customer Goals**: Satisfaction, retention, acquisition targets
- **Innovation Goals**: New products, services, or capabilities

### 5. Action Plan
- **Priorities**: Top 3-5 strategic initiatives for the next year
- **Resources**: Budget, team, and tools needed
- **Timeline**: Key milestones and deadlines
- **Metrics**: How will you measure success?

### 6. Review Process
- **Monthly**: Review progress on key metrics
- **Quarterly**: Assess strategic initiatives and adjust as needed
- **Annually**: Comprehensive strategic plan review and update

This framework helps ensure your business strategy is comprehensive, actionable, and aligned with your long-term vision.
