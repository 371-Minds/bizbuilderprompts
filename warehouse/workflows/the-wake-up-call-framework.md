# The Wake-Up Call Framework

> **Goal:** Break through emotional numbness and reignite the urgency to reclaim your life.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** awakening, emotional-numbness, clarity, urgency, self-reclamation

---

## Methodology

Work through these five steps in order. Step 1: Confront the patterns that keep you emotionally shut down—name the behaviors, habits, and avoidances you use to stay numb. Step 2: Identify the single moment when you stopped fighting for your dreams; describe what happened and what you decided in that moment. Step 3: Reconnect to a memory where you felt truly alive; replay its sensory details. Step 4: Find one way to reignite that same energy in your current life, starting with a small concrete action. Step 5: End by writing a one-line jolt of clarity and urgency that you will use to reclaim your life today.
