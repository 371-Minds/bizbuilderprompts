# Story-first proof

> **Goal:** Use a real story that contains the proof, then state the lesson, to make advice feel lived-in.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** storytelling, proof, content

---

## Methodology

1. Open with a real story that shows the struggle, the decision, and the result. 2. Name the specific lever that changed the outcome. 3. State the lesson immediately after the story. 4. Turn the lesson into a repeatable step the reader can copy. 5. Keep the story short and let the proof do the work.
