# The Fear to Fuel Framework

> **Goal:** Turn anxiety and fear into relentless forward motion through truth flips and actions.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** fear, anxiety, truth-flip, ritual, action

---

## Methodology

Step 1: Write out the fears that keep you frozen. Step 2: For each fear, create a truth flip—a statement that reclaims power from fear. Step 3: Give one action per fear that proves it wrong. Step 4: Design a ritual that turns fear into fire every time you feel it. Step 5: End by feeling in control, not ruled by fear.
