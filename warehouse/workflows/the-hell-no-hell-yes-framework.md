# The Hell No / Hell Yes Framework

> **Goal:** Cut through confusion and make decisions with clarity by using your gut response as a filter.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** decision-filter, gut-instinct, clarity, boldness, hell-yes

---

## Methodology

Step 1: Use your gut response as a compass. Step 2: List five decisions you are avoiding and run each through the Hell No / Hell Yes filter: if it is not a hell yes, it is a hell no. Step 3: Take action on one today without second-guessing. Step 4: End by feeling bold and mentally clear.
