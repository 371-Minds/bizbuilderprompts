# Promise-driven opening

> **Goal:** Write openings that commit readers by leading with the exact outcome, named reader, and a hint of method.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** copywriting, opening, conversion

---

## Methodology

1. Lead with the specific outcome the reader will get. 2. Name the reader in the promise. 3. Hint at your method or proof. 4. Add a time bound if it helps, e.g., 'In the next 5 minutes...' 5. Use that promise as the standard for every section that follows. Example: 'In the next 5 minutes, you’ll learn 3 subject lines that doubled replies for my last campaign, plus the template.'
