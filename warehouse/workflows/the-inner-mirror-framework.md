# The Inner Mirror Framework

> **Goal:** Conduct a brutally honest self-assessment and define the identity you must embody now.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** self-assessment, identity, honesty, gap-analysis, transformation

## Methodology

Step 1: Lead yourself through a brutal yet honest self-assessment of where you are today. Step 2: Ask questions that expose the gap between your current self and your ideal self. Step 3: Forgive the version of you that coped instead of conquered. Step 4: Define the new identity you must embody starting now. Step 5: End by stripping away every lie and reconnecting with truth.
