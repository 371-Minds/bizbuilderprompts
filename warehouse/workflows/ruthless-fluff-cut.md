# Ruthless fluff cut

> **Goal:** Make every line earn its place by deleting greetings, repeats, and vague claims in the first pass.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** editing, concision, writing

---

## Methodology

1. Read the draft as a skimmer. 2. Delete greetings, repeats, and vague claims. 3. Cut every phrase that doesn't move the reader to the next step. 4. Replace filler with a direct promise like 'Do this to get X.' 5. Apply the same pass to product pages, support replies, and meeting notes.
