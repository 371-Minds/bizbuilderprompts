# From Script to Cinema: The 4-Step Pipeline

> **Goal:** Transform a long-form script into a high-fidelity cinematic video through autonomous orchestration.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** video-production, modular-rendering, workflow, synthesis

---

## Methodology

Step 1: Script Ingestion & Segmentation – Accept a long-form script and decompose it into 5-second segments based on narrative beats.
Step 2: Narrative Audit & Refinement – Analyze segments for pace and 'vibe-breaks'; adjust scene duration if text is too dense; optionally inject mythological archetypes.
Step 3: Parallel Render & Generation – Distribute segments to worker nodes to render motion graphics, visuals, and TTS audio simultaneously.
Step 4: Final Concatenation – Assemble all rendered chunks into a single video file using lossless concatenation (e.g., ffmpeg concat) to preserve quality.
