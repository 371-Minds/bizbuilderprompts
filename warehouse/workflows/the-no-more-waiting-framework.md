# The No More Waiting Framework

> **Goal:** Break the habit of delaying your own healing by exposing readiness lies and taking a micro-action.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** procrastination, readiness, micro-action, narrative-shift, decision

---

## Methodology

Step 1: Expose the three lies you keep telling yourself about why you are not ready. Step 2: See what it is costing you to wait. Step 3: Create a micro-action you can do today that signals to the world that you have begun. Step 4: Rewrite your inner narrative from victim to self-rescuer. Step 5: End by feeling you have crossed the threshold from delay to decision.
