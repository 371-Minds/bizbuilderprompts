# One-sentence audience definition

> **Goal:** Target every content decision by defining who you write for in one sentence.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** audience, positioning, content-strategy

---

## Methodology

1. Write one sentence naming the person, their situation, and their goal. 2. Use it as a filter for stories, tone, examples, and detail level. 3. Ask before each line: does this serve that person? 4. Apply the same sentence to offers, emails, and product priorities. Example: 'busy freelance designers who want to raise rates without sounding salesy.'
