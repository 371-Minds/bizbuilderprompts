# The Emotional GPS Framework

> **Goal:** Use avoided emotions as directional signals that guide rather than paralyze you.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** emotions, guidance, avoidance, courage, self-awareness

---

## Methodology

Step 1: List the emotions you try hardest to avoid. Step 2: Decode what those emotions are trying to teach you. Step 3: Choose one emotion to confront and explore in depth. Step 4: Take one courageous action based on that message. Step 5: End by feeling guided, not paralyzed, by emotion.
