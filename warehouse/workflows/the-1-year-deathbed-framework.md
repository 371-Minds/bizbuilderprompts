# The 1-Year Deathbed Framework

> **Goal:** Make choices with the urgency of a one-year timeline and create a priority list that matters now.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** deathbed, prioritization, urgency, purpose, decision-making

---

## Methodology

Step 1: Imagine you have 12 months to live, with full health and freedom. Step 2: Write what you would stop tolerating immediately. Step 3: Write what you would finally start doing. Step 4: Create a top-three priority list to live like it matters now. Step 5: End by feeling urgency, not anxiety, and ready to live on purpose.
