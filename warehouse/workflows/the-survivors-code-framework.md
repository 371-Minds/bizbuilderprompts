# The Survivor's Code Framework

> **Goal:** Turn every scar and survival story into a daily personal creed that builds pride.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** survivor-story, strength, creed, reframing, healing

---

## Methodology

Step 1: Write out the story of what you have survived, no matter how messy. Step 2: Identify the hidden strength you gained from each wound. Step 3: Reframe pain as proof of power, not weakness. Step 4: Turn that story into a personal creed you can repeat every day. Step 5: End by feeling pride, not shame, about your journey.
