# AI as structure generator

> **Goal:** Delegate skeleton work to AI — outlines, section logic, angle lists — while the substance, judgment, and proof stay human.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** ai-collaboration, outlining, process, division-of-labor

---

## Methodology

1. Split the job before prompting: structure (how the argument is arranged) is delegable; substance (what is claimed and whether it's true) is not. Write this split down — it's the contract for the whole collaboration.
2. Ask AI for structures, plural: "give me five different section orders for this material, each with a one-line rationale for why a reader would prefer it." You're mining options, not accepting an answer.
3. Feed it your raw material — claims, stories, data fragments — and ask for arrangement options only, with an explicit instruction not to add claims. Anything new it introduces anyway gets quarantined into a "to verify" list, never into the draft.
4. Choose and edit the skeleton yourself. Merge two options, reorder sections, delete the AI's connective logic and keep only the section purposes. The chosen structure is now yours.
5. Draft the substance into that skeleton with whatever mix of human writing and tightly-scoped AI passes suits you — each pass still governed by the same split.
6. Credit the division in your process notes: structure-AI-assisted, substance-human. Provenance of the workflow keeps the honest record straight for later audits.

Used this way, AI functions as a fast junior editor for arrangement problems — and never as the author of record.
