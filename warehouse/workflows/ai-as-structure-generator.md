# AI as structure generator

> **Goal:** Keep your personal voice by using AI to produce outlines, sections, and bullet points, then rewriting in your own words.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** ai-workflow, voice, writing

---

## Methodology

1. Ask AI for a strong headline, sections, and bullet points on your topic. 2. Ask it to include objections and a checklist. 3. Take the structure as a scaffold. 4. Rewrite each section in your own words, adding your stories, client phrases, and opinions. 5. Never publish AI's final wording verbatim.
