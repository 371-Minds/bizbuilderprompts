# The Inner Critic Interrogation Framework

> **Goal:** Put your self-attacking voice on trial and build a rebuttal that defends your truth and value.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** inner-critic, rebuttal, self-worth, limiting-beliefs, questioning

---

## Methodology

Step 1: Identify your most frequent internal insults or limiting beliefs. Step 2: For each one, ask: Who first said this to me—and were they even right? Step 3: Write a rebuttal that defends your truth and value. Step 4: End by feeling louder than your inner critic.
