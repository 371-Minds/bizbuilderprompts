# Picky editor gap check

> **Goal:** Run the draft through an AI editor configured to find what's missing — unanswered questions, skipped steps, undefined terms.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** ai-collaboration, editing, quality-control, completeness

---

## Methodology

1. Configure the editor persona precisely: "You are a meticulous commissioning editor who rejects drafts for missing prerequisites, undefined terms, and logical jumps. You never comment on style." Style comments dilute the pass.
2. Submit the draft with the audience sentence and the one takeaway, and ask exclusively for gaps: what a first-time reader would not understand, what steps are assumed, what terms are used before they're defined.
3. Convert every note into a binary decision — fill it or explicitly cut the section that needs it. A gap you won't fill is a section you can't keep; "readers will figure it out" is not a third option.
4. Rank the remaining gaps by reader impact, then fill the biggest with your own material first. Where you draft with AI assistance, fill one gap at a time so each addition stays scoped and checkable.
5. Re-run the pass on the amended draft. New content creates new gaps; the loop closes only when the editor's notes turn to nitpicks below your quality bar.
6. Log the final gap list in the piece's notes. The pattern of your own gaps — usually prerequisites or definitions — is your personal writing blind spot, worth knowing.

This pass catches the failures readers actually bounce on: not bad sentences, but missing ones.
