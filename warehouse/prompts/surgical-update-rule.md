# Surgical Update Rule

> **Goal:** Efficiently revise a modular video project without re-rendering the entire sequence.
> **Framework:** kaizen
> **Tags:** iterative-rendering, modular-workflows, efficiency

---

## Methodology

When a revision is needed in a project composed of independent 5-second chunks, re-render only the specific chunk(s) that changed. Leave the rest of the project untouched to preserve the original quality and save time.
