# Give AI audience context upfront

> **Goal:** Make AI output match real intent by defining the reader, their goal, level, and situation before requesting content.
> **Framework:** custom
> **Tags:** ai-prompts, audience, context

---

## Methodology

Before every AI request, paste this context: 'Audience is first time Etsy sellers. They have 2 hours a week. They fear tech. Goal is 10 listings. Tone is friendly and direct. Context is a 7 day challenge.' Then ask for practical steps and language that fits that reader. Also state the format, length, and proof you need.
